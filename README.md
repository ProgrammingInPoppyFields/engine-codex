## Overview

The Post-Primal Liturgy is a mythos experiment: a growing codex of symbolic structures observed in the aftermath of modern life, cosmic scale, memory, material pressure, signal, grief, transformation, and ordinary dread.

The entries in this archive are not gods.  
They are not characters.  
They are not metaphors alone.

They are Engines: recurring structures of meaning given body, material, signal, habitat, relation, and aftereffect.

Some build.  
Some transmit.  
Some endure.  
Some remember.  
Some fail to become stable enough for polite taxonomy.

The Codex exists for browsing, cross-reference, containment, and public observation.

Readers are advised not to linger.

---

## Primary Classifications

### Engines

Stable phenomena with enough symbolic gravity to alter the field around them.

### Drives

Engine-like forces defined more by motion, pressure, compulsion, or transformation than by fixed architecture.

### Engine Groups

Clustered structures best understood as sets, trios, monoliths, or related operating states.

### Unresolved Entities

Proto-Engines, fallen forms, parasites, restricted phenomena, or symbolic structures that failed to achieve Engine integrity.
