## Classification

**Engine**  
**Material Engine**

## Material / Color

Ceramic: fired clay, porcelain, glaze, ballistic plate, tile, vessel, shard.

CERAMIC carries the contradiction of domestic fragility and engineered resistance. It can be cup, altar, bathroom tile, dinner plate, spacecraft shield, armor plate, heirloom, weapon fragment.

Its color ranges from warm clay to white glaze to tactical matte.

## Signal Profile

Fragility hardened by fire. Domestic object under threat. Beauty with a fracture line.

CERAMIC broadcasts the unstable truth that the delicate and the defensive are often the same body under different conditions.

## Sound Signature

A spoon against porcelain.

Kiln heat. Plate crack. A small ringing impact that makes everyone in the room briefly aware of breakability.

## Profile

CERAMIC is the Engine of fired contradiction.

It begins soft, takes shape, enters heat, and emerges hardened — not invincible, but changed. Its strength is real. Its brittleness is also real. Neither cancels the other.

CERAMIC belongs to kitchens and armor systems, ritual vessels and shattered plates, tiled bathrooms and heat shields. It is intimate and technical, fragile and resistant, everyday and extreme.

This Engine does not resolve the contradiction.

It holds it.

CERAMIC knows that hardness can be produced by vulnerability passing through fire. It also knows that hardened things can still break.
