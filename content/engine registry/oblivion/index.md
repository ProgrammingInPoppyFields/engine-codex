# THE OBLIVION ENGINE

![THE OBLIVION ENGINE](/images/oblivion/1.png)

> No plaque. No engraving. No measurable trace.

## Aliases

- OBLIVION
- The Null Memorial
- The Recordless Engine
- The Erasure Drive

## Classification

**Engine**  
**Aftermath Engine**

## Material / Color

Perfectly smooth black marble.

No plaque.  
No engraving.  
No visible imperfection.

OBLIVION is made from memorial material stripped of memorial function. It is stone without inscription, monument without name, record without content.

Its color is absolute polished black.

## Signal Profile

Total historical null. Trace failure. Memory with nothing left to hold.

OBLIVION broadcasts the silence of the dead who leave no measurable mark in the historical record.

## Sound Signature

Nothing inscribing.

A chisel failing against a surface. A tool lifted away. Silence so smooth it rejects echo.

## Profile

OBLIVION represents the dead who leave no measurable trace in the historical record.

Unlike SECOND DEATH, which concerns names disappearing from living memory, OBLIVION is more absolute. There may be no name to lose, no photograph to stop circulating, no obituary to search, no identifiable trace to preserve.

Attempts to inscribe the surface have failed.

The stone remains perfectly smooth.

It is related to DUST, but without DUST’s sentimental cosmic mercy.

Same outcome.

Fewer gestures.

## Habitats
