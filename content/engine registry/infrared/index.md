## Material / Color

Thermal radiation, heat map gradient, remote control LED, night-vision green, and the red-orange glow of bodies revealing themselves to instruments.

INFRARED is made of heat leaving surfaces. Its material is the radiation that every warm body emits constantly and involuntarily — invisible to the eye, legible to the right sensor. Its color is the false-color palette of thermal imaging: white for hot, black for cold, and every shade of betrayal in between.

## Signal Profile

Heat signature. Remote command. Warmth made visible. Short-range invisible instruction.

THE INFRARED ENGINE governs the radiation of heat as information — bodies, machines, and environments revealing themselves through temperature.

## Sound Signature

The click of a remote control. The faint whirr of a thermal camera focusing. The silence of a heat signature being recorded. No sound from the radiation itself — only the instruments that read it.

## Profile

INFRARED is the Engine of thermal revelation: the invisible light that makes warmth legible.

Every living body emits infrared radiation constantly. Every engine, every wall, every cup of coffee, every human being is broadcasting its temperature into the environment at all times. Infrared does not require cooperation. It reads you whether you consent or not.

In its domestic form, infrared is the remote control — a short-range pulse of invisible light carrying a command from hand to device. Point, press, instruct. The device obeys without contact.

In its surveillance form, infrared is thermal imaging — the technology that sees bodies through darkness, walls, and weather. Search and rescue. Military targeting. Building inspection. Medical diagnostics. In each case, the Engine's principle is the same: heat betrays presence.

INFRARED is the Engine of involuntary visibility. You are always warm. You are always broadcasting.
