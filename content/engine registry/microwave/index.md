## Profile

MICROWAVE is the Engine of invisible agitation: the electromagnetic frequency that heats matter without touching it, transmits data across distances, and detects objects by their reflection.

In its domestic form, the microwave oven is perhaps the strangest appliance in any kitchen. It does not burn. It does not conduct. It instructs water molecules to vibrate at a specific frequency until the substance they inhabit becomes hot. It heats from the inside out. It requires no flame, no element, no contact. It is radiation made mundane.

In its telecommunications form, microwaves carry point-to-point signals, satellite uplinks, and wireless backhaul. They are the frequency band that bridges gaps between towers, stations, and orbital infrastructure.

In its detection form, microwaves overlap with radar — the same frequencies, the same physics, directed differently.

MICROWAVE is the Engine of energy applied as instruction. It does not ask matter to cooperate. It simply tells it to move.
