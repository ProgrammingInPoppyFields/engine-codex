# THE SIGNAL ENGINE

![THE SIGNAL ENGINE](/images/signal/20.png)

> Presence trying to be received.

## Aliases

- SIGNAL
- The Engine of Presence
- The Seeking Signal

## Classification

**Engine**  
**Emanation Engine**

## Material / Color

Light, radio, pulse, notification glow, antenna geometry, waveform, and reaching presence.

SIGNAL is made of directed emission: not message content, not story, not broadcast narrative, but the fact of reaching outward at all.

Its color is often white-blue, screen-lit, electric, and lonely.

## Signal Profile

Presence seeking reception. The need to be detected.

SIGNAL broadcasts the simplest and most devastating claim: **I am here.**

## Sound Signature

A ping.

A pulse tone. Notification chime from another room. Radio static resolving briefly into proof of someone trying.

## Profile

SIGNAL is the Engine of presence: the act of trying to become detectable.

It is not the message itself. That belongs closer to BROADCAST. SIGNAL is more primitive and more intimate. It says only that something is here, something is emitting, something wants to be received.

SIGNAL is contemporary because modern life is full of pings, read receipts, typing indicators, location dots, emergency alerts, weak Wi-Fi bars, missed calls, blue bubbles, satellite contact, and machines asking whether anyone is still there.

It operates at multiple scales.

A phone notification in a dark bedroom.  
A tower blinking over a city.  
A planetary signal leaving the atmosphere.

At every scale, the question remains the same.

Did anyone receive it?
