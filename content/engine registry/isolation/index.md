# THE ISOLATION ENGINE

![THE ISOLATION ENGINE](/images/136.png)

> Nothing grows here. Nothing calls back.

## Aliases

- ISOLATION
- Terminal Habitat Failure
- The Terminal Distance Engine
- The Vacuum Drive

## Classification

**Engine**  
**Aftermath Engine**

## Material / Color

Dead asteroid, vacuum, cold rock, no atmosphere, no biosphere, no signal activity.

ISOLATION is not island-stone. It is not living terrain. It is not a lighthouse-bearing place of constructive solitude.

It is inert mass drifting through nothing.

Its color is starless black, dead gray, and the absence of any warm signal.

## Signal Profile

Separation without habitat. Solitude with no life-supporting function.

ISOLATION broadcasts the failure of distance to become refuge.

## Sound Signature

Vacuum.

No beacon tone. No wind. No surf. No returned call. No atmospheric medium for comfort to travel through.

## Profile

ISOLATION is the Engine of terminal habitat failure.

It appears as a dead asteroid drifting indefinitely through vacuum. No atmosphere. No biosphere. No signal activity.

Unlike ISLAND, this structure sustains nothing.

ISLAND builds the lighthouse first.

ISOLATION refuses to return anyone’s calls.

Long-term observation confirms no change.
