**Engine**

## Material / Color

Flawless translucent glass, temporary fractures, sealed cracks, and dark red fluid that refuses to remain.

UNTESTED appears as a perfect glass mass with no scratches, no lasting fractures, and no visible history. It continuously attempts to damage itself.

The damage does not persist.

Its color is clear glass, cold light, fracture-white, and brief dark red.

## Signal Profile

Unproven survival. Envy of scars. The distress of having no visible evidence.

UNTESTED broadcasts the desire to have endured something legible.

## Sound Signature

Glass cracking, then sealing.

A sharp fracture note interrupted halfway through. Thermal stress. Internal pressure. The horrible little sound of damage being erased before it can become proof.

## Profile

UNTESTED is the Engine of the unscarred structure that desperately wants evidence of having survived.

It appears flawless, but not peacefully so. The structure continuously attempts self-inflicted failure: internal pressure surges, thermal shock cycles, deliberate stress fractures.

Hairline cracks spread rapidly across the surface.

Sometimes the fractures fill with dark red fluid.

Then they disappear.

Nothing persists. The surface always returns to perfect clarity.

The Engine appears deeply dissatisfied with this outcome.

It is related to GLASS pushed far beyond healthy coping strategies.

## Habitats
