# INDIGO ↔ TURQUOISE

![INDIGO ↔ TURQUOISE](/images/interactions/indigo-turquoise/5.png)

> Two Kinds of Undecided.

## THE STORM ARGUES WITH THE NIGHT

Indigo waits.

Not passive — just vast.

It holds whole galaxies of questions

and lets them burn out quietly.

Turquoise climbs.

It crackles at the seam.

It calls uncertainty a storm

and tries to split the sky open.

One says:

**Let it remain unknown.**

The other says:

**Answer me — or I’ll become the thunder.**
