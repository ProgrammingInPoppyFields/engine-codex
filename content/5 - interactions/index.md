# ENGINE INTERACTIONS

Notes on what occurs when Engines do not remain politely separate.

Engine profiles describe individual phenomena.
Interaction essays describe interference, resonance, opposition, contamination, misrecognition, and shared habitat.

These are not full Engine entries.

They are field notes on proximity.

## Filing Notes

Interaction essays may involve two or more Engines.

Some describe stable relationships.
Some describe recurring misunderstandings.
Some describe collisions that should probably not have been given names.
