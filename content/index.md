## Primary Classifications

**Engines** are major symbolic structures with stable domains.

**Drives** are Engine-like forces defined more by movement, pressure, or compulsion than by fixed architecture.

**Engine Families** are clustered systems: related Engines or states that operate best as a set.

**Unresolved Entities** are proto-Engines, fallen forms, parasites, restricted phenomena, or things that refused clean classification.
