# THE SARCOPHAGUS OPENS

![](/images/473.png)

> WHEN UNBOWED OPENS

When UNBOWED opens,
it does not reveal a wound.

It reveals distance.

The stone gives way
with the patience
of something that has waited
too long
to be asked correctly.

Blackened limestone.
Sarcophagus dark.
A standing slab
everyone mistook
for discipline.

Then the seam widens.

And inside:

not a body.

Not ashes.

Not the small, acceptable room
where suffering is supposed
to keep itself.

Inside is depth.

A chamber without walls.
A night without ceiling.
A vaulted dark
large enough
to make the monument
look like a keyhole.

Constellations hang
where ribs should be.

Slow galaxies turn
in the places
no one thought
to check.

There are stars
made from every day
it did not collapse.

There are nebulae
the color of held breath.

There are black halls
of unsaid anger,
lit from very far away
by tiny, furious suns.

You understand then
why it stayed vertical.

Not because it was fine.

Not because it was noble.

Not because endurance
is a pleasant shape
for other people
to admire.

It stood
because something vast
was inside it.

It stood
because no one noticed
the sky it was carrying.

It stood
because collapse
would have been called tragedy,

but containment
was called strength.

When UNBOWED opens,
you do not find emptiness.

You find infinity
kept in the dark
without witness.

You find the proof
that something can look upright
from the outside

and still contain
an entire night sky
waiting for recognition.
