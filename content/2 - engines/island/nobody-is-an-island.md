# NOBODY IS AN ISLAND

![](/images/island/5.png)

> ISLAND ENGINE ↔ ISOLATION ENGINE

The beaches narrow.

The birds leave.

The bridges rot

before they can

reach anyone.

At last,

if no one comes,

if nothing crosses

the water,

if every signal

dies

in the weather

before reply,

the Island Engine

begins,

slowly,

to resemble

something else.

Not a fortress.

Not a weapon.

Not a thing made

cruel by neglect.

Something quieter.

A terminal habitat

drifting inward on

itself.

This is the early stages

of the ISOLATION Engine.

The atmosphere thins.

The crossings stop.

The lights go dark

one practical failure

at a time.

And still

it does not hate the sea.

That is the saddest part.

It does not poison

the water.

It does not sharpen

into bitterness.

It does not learn

revenge.

It simply becomes

less alive.

A green place gone grey.

A shelter with no sleepers.

A stubborn, exhausted rock

in dark water,

still facing the world,

still refusing

to make it worse,

but no longer able

to make it home.
