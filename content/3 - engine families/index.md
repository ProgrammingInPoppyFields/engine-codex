# ENGINE FAMILIES

> Some phenomena do not appear alone.

Engine Families are clustered structures: sets of Engines, Drives, or states that become clearer when read together.

A group is not always a hierarchy.  
It is not always a family.  
It is not always peaceful.

Sometimes a group is a spectrum.  
Sometimes it is a trinity.  
Sometimes it is four monoliths standing too close together in the dark.

## Current Group Types

**Cognitive groups** describe internal states, operating modes, and the fracture between feeling, function, and mechanism.

**Material groups** describe related forms of matter, residue, pressure, or finality.

**Monolithic groups** describe large-scale structures too massive or abstract to behave like ordinary Engine entries.

## How to Read Groups

Group pages may work in one of two ways.

Some groups contain all member descriptions in a single page.

Others act as a hub, with each member receiving its own full profile.

If this seems inconsistent, that is because the Codex is descriptive, not moral.

It records the shape that best contains the phenomenon.
