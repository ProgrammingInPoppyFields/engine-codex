## Profile

The Particulate Finalities are a three-member Engine group: **SAND**, **ASH**, and **DUST**.

They are not the same ending.

SAND is erosion and abrasion: loud, young, bright, restless, granular.  
ASH is the residue of burning: grief after heat, evidence after flame.  
DUST is the eldest finality: time reduced to smallest matter, memory dispersed, stardust returning to surface.

Together, they describe the small materials left when larger forms fail, burn, weather, die, or become history.

They are finalities because they remain after the event stops being dramatic.
