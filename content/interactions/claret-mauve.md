# CLARET ↔ MAUVE

> TWO STARS DISCUSS VISIBILITY

![](/images/450.png)

CLARET says:

I am still burning.

MAUVE says:

I know.

CLARET says:

Then why does no one see me?

MAUVE dims
behind a veil
of its own carbon dust.

Not dead.

Not smaller.

Only harder
to prove.

CLARET flares red-purple,
young enough
to still believe
brightness is an argument.

MAUVE answers
from farther inside
the same color:

Wait long enough
and even fire
learns to lower its voice.

CLARET says:

That sounds like surrender.

MAUVE says:

No.

It is what burning does
when visibility
becomes unreliable.

Between them,
space remains black.

No witness arrives.

No telescope turns.

Still,
both stars continue—

one vivid with almost,

one faded with almost,

each carrying
the same wound

at a different
distance from belief.
