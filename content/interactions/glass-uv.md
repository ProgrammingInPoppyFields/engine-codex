# GLASS ↔ ULTRAVIOLET

![GLASS ↔ ULTRAVIOLET](/images/123.jpg)

> Spectral Reveal

## THE QUIET THAT SEES, AND THE LIGHT THAT ISN’T SEEN

UV is already everywhere

a pressure no eye admits

GLASS doesn’t add anything

it only reveals what was there

The beam passes through

and suddenly the invisible has edges

UV hums:

**You will be forgotten.**

GLASS answers:

**Not while light can split.**
