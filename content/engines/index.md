# ENGINES

![ENGINES](/images/engines/1.png)

> Stable phenomena with enough symbolic gravity to alter the field around them.

Engines are the primary structures of the Codex.

Each Engine governs a domain: origin, endurance, isolation, signal, matter, memory, transformation, aftermath, or some other pressure large enough to take form.

They are not characters.  
They are not gods.  
They are not metaphors alone.

They are recurring structures of meaning given body, material, signal, habitat, and aftereffect.

## What an Engine Entry Contains

Most Engine pages include:

- **Material / Color** — what the Engine appears to be made of
- **Signal Profile** — what emotional or conceptual pressure it broadcasts
- **Sound Signature** — what it seems to sound like nearby
- **Habitats** — where it tends to appear
- **Aftereffects** — what remains after contact
- **Relations / Linked Entries** — nearby Engines, Drives, entities, or failed forms

## Engine Families

Some Engines belong to loose families or tags:

- **Existence Engines**
- **Color Engines**
- **Material Engines**
- **Emanation Engines**
- **Aftermath Engines**
- **Contemporary Engines**

These families are navigational aids, not commandments.

The Engines are not particularly obedient.
