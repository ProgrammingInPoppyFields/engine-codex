# THE EMANATION ENGINES

> Presence leaving itself.

- The Signal Family
- The Transmission Engines

## Classification

**Engine Family**

## Family Principle

The Emanation Engines govern presence that leaves its source.

Not all emanation is broadcast. Not all signal is speech.

Some signals ask to be received. Some signals flood the room. Some search for a return. Some travel by glass, heat, tower, orbit, or paired proximity.

In this family, SIGNAL and BROADCAST are treated as behavioral principles:

**SIGNAL** is presence seeking reception.  
**BROADCAST** is one-to-many outward transmission.

The individual Engines below are the technologies, fields, and rituals through which those behaviors become physically and symbolically distinct.
